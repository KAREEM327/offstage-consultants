What to bring

The review is not a demo and not a sales call. It is an hour of pressure-testing. Come with:

  • Your completed scores — all 22, including the ones you guessed at.
  • The controls you scored a 2 but aren't sure about. These are the most valuable minutes of the call. Self-reports are generous; that is the whole reason this session exists.
  • Your rough architecture — where agents run, what they can reach, what stores the audit trail. A napkin sketch is enough.
  • The deadline driving this, if there is one. A security review, a client launch, an audit. It changes what we tell you to fix first.

Run the assessment before the call. If you show up without scores we will spend the hour doing what you could have done in 45 minutes on your own — and you'll get a generic answer instead of yours.

What you'll leave with

  • A corrected score — the 2s that are really 1s, named.
  • A remediation order specific to your architecture, not the generic blast-radius list in the kit.
  • A straight answer on whether your current posture survives a customer's security review, and what the honest claim is until it does.

If it turns out the gap is bigger than an hour can close, we'll say so and point you at the AI Governance Audit. We won't spend the call selling it.
